Getting coverage across a corpus nobody has time to annotate by hand — and getting a corpus that has
never been measured onto a ratchet without failing every page on day one.

This section is planned and not yet written. It will cover:

- `docevals fill` — proposing evals from page content, gated on a confidence threshold
- Seeing the cost before spending it, and why re-gating at a different threshold is free
- Retrofitting a legacy corpus: lower the severity, never weaken the assertion
- `docevals promote` and `docevals generate` — moving evals down the grader hierarchy
- Reviewing generated check scripts as ordinary source

Until then, the [README](https://github.com/hawkeyexl/docevals#readme) describes `fill`, `promote`,
and `generate`.
