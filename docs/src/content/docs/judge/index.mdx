The answer to "why should a model be allowed to block our pipeline?" — the reproducibility
guarantees, the ensemble, the confidence zones, and the calibration numbers that make the case.

This section is planned and not yet written. It will cover:

- What makes a verdict reproducible: temperature 0, pinned models, structured verdicts, caching
- The ensemble and consensus — including that a `partial` verdict counts as a fail, and that errored
  runs count against consensus rather than toward a silent pass
- Confidence zones: auto-pass, auto-fail, and the human-review band between them
- Calibrating against a human-verified golden set, and watching the false-positive rate
- Choosing a provider, including options that need no API key or that stay on your own infrastructure

Until then, the [README](https://github.com/hawkeyexl/docevals#readme) covers providers and
calibration.
